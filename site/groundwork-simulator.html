What is Groundwork Simulator?

Groundwork Simulator is an open-world heavy machinery and gold-mining simulation from XYZ STUDIO. Players operate excavators, wheel loaders, bulldozers, drills and transport vehicles while reshaping terrain, moving material and building a working wash plant.

The game supports solo play and online co-op for up to four players. Terrain, mud, slopes, machine hydraulics and water supply are parts of the simulation rather than background decoration.

The confirmed gameplay loop

1

Find deposits

Use a drill rig to test the ground and choose a promising excavation site.

2

Dig and haul

Cut into the terrain, load trucks and keep material moving across the site.

3

Wash for gold

Route water through pumps and hoses, then feed the wash plant and sluice boxes.

4

Expand the site

Add machinery, improve access routes and coordinate work with other players.

What is planned after Early Access?

  1. Update 1: a new map and additional story objectives.
  2. Update 2: more vehicles, wash plants, processing equipment and deeper controls.
  3. Full release: mod support, modding tutorials, Map Studio, performance work and fixes for progression-blocking bugs.

The developer describes these as plans rather than guaranteed dates or final features.
